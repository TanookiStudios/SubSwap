    About

    I built this because I was moving to a new YouTube account and realized the alternative was clicking Subscribe about five hundred times. I got roughly forty in before deciding there's gotta be a better way. I present to you, my better way.

    I'm Maddie, I'm autistic, and I often make small tools that fix one specific irritation properly — the sort of thing that's too small for a company to bother with and too annoying to keep putting up with.

    I also develop apps for PC, Mac, mobiles, and tablets. I generally develop apps that:

    1. Help me with a problem that I have.
    2. Will help others.
    3. Bring a little bit of good to the world.

    This is also my income. Due to my autism, I don't do well with most typical office jobs. In fact, I tend to really struggle. So with the support of my wife, I decided to invest in myself, and create my own apps full-time.

    Support My Work

    SubSwap is free and it stays free. There's no paid tier, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is held back until you pay — what you've got is all of it. It's open source under the MIT license, so you don't have to take my word for any of that.

    Tips are the whole business model. There's nothing here to buy and nothing to upgrade to, so if these tools are worth something to you, this is the only way to say so.
