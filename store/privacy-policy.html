Privacy

SubSwap collects nothing.

Last updated October 6, 2026

SubSwap has no account, no server and no analytics. Nothing it reads about you ever leaves your browser. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What SubSwap stores

Two things, both in your browser's own local extension storage:

  • The list of YouTube channels you chose to save — the channel name, its ID and its address.
  • The progress of a run in flight, so that if you close the browser you can carry on where you left off.

That's it. No email address, no name, no identifiers, no browsing history, no viewing history.

Where it goes

Nowhere. There is no server to send it to. SubSwap makes no network requests of its own — it reads pages you are already signed into and are already looking at, and everything it works out stays on your machine. The one exception is the tip jar, described below, and nothing happens there unless you press the button yourself.

If you press Save to a file, the file is written to your computer by your browser. What you then do with that file is entirely up to you.

What it can see, and when

SubSwap can read pages on youtube.com and nowhere else. It reads your subscriptions page when you ask it to save your list, and it reads a channel page to check whether you are already subscribed before doing anything there. It doesn't watch what you browse, and it does nothing at all until you press a button.

The tip jar

The Support My Work section has a form on it for leaving a tip. It is entirely optional, nothing in SubSwap is locked behind it, and the extension works exactly the same whether you use it or not.

If you press Chip In, a new tab opens and the amount you picked is sent to tanookistudios.com, which hands you to Stripe to take the payment. That request carries the amount and nothing else — no list of channels, no progress, no identifier, nothing at all about your YouTube account. Your card details go to Stripe and are handled under Stripe's privacy policy; I never see them, and no record of who gave money is kept anywhere outside Stripe.

Third parties

Stripe, and only if you choose to leave a tip, as above. Nothing is sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising, because nothing is collected in the first place. There are no third-party libraries, trackers or scripts in the extension — the tip form is plain HTML that posts when you press it, which is why there is no Stripe code running inside SubSwap at all.

Removing your data

Delete a saved list from the extension and it is gone immediately. Uninstall the extension and your browser removes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else for us to delete, and nothing to request.

Children

SubSwap isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes — and it would only change if the extension itself gained a feature that needed it — the updated policy will appear at this address with a new date, and the change will be described in the extension's release notes.

Contact

Questions about any of this can go to the support address on the Chrome Web Store listing.
